📲 Install StockTrack App
🌐 Samsung Internet Browser (Android) 1. Tap the bottom right menu (≡) icon.
2. Tap “Add page to”.
3. Choose “App screen” (or “Home screen”).
🌐 Google Chrome (Android / Phone) 1. Tap top right three dots (⋮).
2. Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
🍏 Apple iPhone / iPad (Safari) 1. Tap the Share button (square with arrow up ⬆).
2. Scroll and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
💻 Laptop / PC (Chrome or Edge) 1. Look at the browser address bar on top right.
2. Click the Install (⊕) icon.
